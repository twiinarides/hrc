Child Reception & Protection — What We Do

Emergency Intake & Reception

We receive children referred by Police, local authorities, hospitals, and community members. Every child is assessed within 24 hours of arrival by qualified social workers.

Immediate Safety & Shelter

Children are provided immediate safe shelter, food, clothing, and a secure environment away from harm, abuse, or neglect.

Health & Medical Support

Comprehensive medical check-ups, nutritional support, and psycho-social first aid are provided immediately upon reception.

Family Tracing & Reunification

Our social work team actively traces family members and works toward safe reunification or alternative placement in line with Uganda's Children Act.

Who Qualifies for Child Reception?

  • Abandoned, orphaned, or unaccompanied children
  • Children rescued from abuse, neglect, or exploitative situations
  • Children in conflict with the law requiring temporary care
  • Refugee children without guardians
  • Children referred by courts, police or MGLSD Uganda

Counseling Services — What to Expect

Individual Counseling

One-on-one sessions with qualified counselors covering trauma healing, grief processing, behavioural challenges, and mental wellness.

Group Therapy Sessions

Facilitated peer support groups allowing children and youth to share experiences, build coping skills and social support networks.

Digital Online Sessions

Video and text-based counseling for clients who cannot attend in-person. Sessions are conducted via secure video call platforms.

Anonymous Support

For those not ready to reveal identity, our anonymous text-based platform allows safe, confidential help-seeking without registration.

Privacy & Confidentiality

All counseling sessions at Hope Reception Centre are strictly confidential. Your information and discussions are protected and will not be shared with any third party without your written consent, except in cases where your safety or another person's safety is at immediate risk.

Education & Vocational Support — What We Offer

School Fees Assistance

We pay primary and secondary school fees for children under our care who cannot afford education. This includes government and private schools in Kabale district.

Learning Materials

School uniforms, books, stationery, and all necessary school materials are provided to enrolled children who cannot afford them.

Vocational Skills Training

Youth who have aged out of primary school receive practical vocational training in carpentry, tailoring, hairdressing, and entrepreneurship to enable self-reliance.

After-School Tutoring

Our staff and volunteers provide extra tutoring support to help children catch up on missed schoolwork, especially new intakes who have been out of school.

Eligibility for Education Support

  • Children residing at Hope Reception Centre
  • Vulnerable children from the wider Kabale community on case-by-case basis
  • Youth aged 14–24 for vocational training programs
  • Children from families below the poverty line referred by local leaders
